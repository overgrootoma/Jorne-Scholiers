Mars Attacks

2024
school assignment

The assignment was to design a brand identity for the Olympic Games on Mars in the year 2096. I developed a concept centered around plants and growth, imagining a future where athletic competition also plays a role in bringing life to Mars.

Since Mars has no oxygen, I built the identity around the idea that athletes are competing not just for medals, but to help the planet, bringing energy, movement, and life through sport.

Each type of Olympic Games: Summer Olympics, Winter Olympics, and Paralympics is represented by a different flower. The visual identity focuses heavily on the theme of growth, with repeating patterns.

In addition to the posters, I also had to design a promotional installation intended to be placed on Earth inviting people to imagine the future games on Mars.

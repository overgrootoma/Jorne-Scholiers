Hotel Identity

2025
school assignment
group project

In this school project, we teamed up in teams of 2 to create a brand identity for a fictional hotel. Our concept centered around a hotel designed specifically for jazz musicians, a place where they can meet, unwind, and get creative energy. We were drawn to the idea because there is no real place like this.

The visual identity blends fluid, expressive lines with bold, vibrant colors. This represents the rhythm and spontaneity of jazz music. We aimed for a balance between the classic elegance of jazz culture and a contemporary design, creating a brand that feels both timeless and fresh.

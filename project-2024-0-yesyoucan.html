YesYouCan

For this school assignment, we worked in duos to design packaging for a fictional drink. The goal was to create a cohesive visual system across three cans, with a strong identity.

We developed "DYNAMO", an energy drink concept designed for extreme dry environments. Each flavor is inspired by a different desert landscape: Dry Desert energy (core flavor), Badlands energy, and Deadvlei energy, with visual elements that reflect the unique textures, colors, and atmosphere of each terrain.

We started the project by individually creating our own concepts, then merged our ideas into one final design. This process allowed us to combine different perspectives and strengths into a single outcome.

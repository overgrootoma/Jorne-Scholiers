Sound Translations of Fungal Forms

2026
school assignment

STFF is an experimental project that uses fungi as a starting point for music creation. Without a background in music, we treated fungal growth as a bridge between ourselves and sound, translating organic forms into visual and musical compositions. Through a custom-built tool, fungal structures were transformed into audio, resulting in a visual album, an immersive installation, and a series of physical artifacts.

Graphic design projects

Sound Translations of Fungal Forms

2026

STFF is an experimental project that uses fungi as a starting point for music creation. Without a background in music, we treated fungal growth as a bridge between ourselves and sound, translating organic forms into visual and musical compositions. Through a custom-built tool, fungal structures were transformed into audio, resulting in a visual album, an immersive installation, and a series of physical artifacts.

Isolation

2026

This book explores isolation both as a visual theme and as a design method. Moving away from traditional design software, I developed a custom tool to generate and transform images. Using 100 photographs sourced from Unsplash through the keyword “isolation,” the tool reconstructs each image into a line-based grid and introduces a force field around the isolated subject, distorting its surroundings while keeping the subject intact. The result is a series of experimental images in which isolation is not only represented, but embedded in the structure of the image itself.

2025

This work was selected by Monar Antwerp and UGG to be presented during Antwerp Art Weekend. These photographs, an abstract experiment with photography, were originally created as part of a school project, but I recognized their potential to become more than just an exercise. Photographs layered over each other create a piece where you can keep finding new details.

Colis Paris

2025

For this assignment, we were asked to design a packet for a Paris-based graphic design studio. I chose Image Format. The packet includes custom-designed packaging, three postcards, a flag, two branded items, and a booklet that reflects the studio's identity and aesthetic. The result is a cohesive and playful package that captures the spirit of Image Format while showcasing my interpretation of their visual identity.

Of All Things: Bord

2025

For this assignment, we were challenged to create a magazine centered around a single object. We chose "board" as the theme, exploring its many interpretations. From snowboards to traffic signs. Through experimental layouts and typography we created an interesting magazine.

Hotel Identity

2025

In this school project, we teamed up in teams of 2 to create a brand identity for a fictional hotel. Our concept centered around a hotel designed specifically for jazz musicians, a place where they can meet, unwind, and get creative energy. We were drawn to the idea because there is no real place like this.

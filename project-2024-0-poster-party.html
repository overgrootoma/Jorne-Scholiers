Poster Party

2024
school assignment

For this assignment, I designed a series of three posters, each with a different theme, along with three accompanying derivatives: a flyer, a sticker, and Instagram posts.

The posters were based on the themes "Museumnacht" (Museum Night), "Inscript", and a quote of our choice.

For "Museumnacht", I was inspired by nightshops and their glowing LED signs.

For "Inscript", I drew inspiration from early 2000s computer interfaces. This because it is a digital event.

For the quote, I chose a statement by Bob Marley on racism. I brought a human and emotional element into the design through handwritten typography and visual references to skin.

Each poster explores a different visual language.

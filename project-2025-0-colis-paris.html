Colis Paris

Packaging and printed matter for a Paris design studio

For Colis Paris, I designed a packet for the Paris-based graphic design studio Image Format. The packet includes custom-designed packaging, three postcards, a flag, two branded items, and a booklet that reflects the studio's identity and aesthetic. The result is a cohesive and playful package that captures the spirit of Image Format while showcasing my interpretation of their visual identity.

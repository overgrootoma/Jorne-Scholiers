Colis Paris

2025
school assignment

For this assignment, we were asked to design a packet for a Paris-based graphic design studio. I chose Image Format. The packet includes custom-designed packaging, three postcards, a flag, two branded items, and a booklet that reflects the studio's identity and aesthetic. The result is a cohesive and playful package that captures the spirit of Image Format while showcasing my interpretation of their visual identity.
